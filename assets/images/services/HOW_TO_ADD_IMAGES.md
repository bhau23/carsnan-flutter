# 📸 How to Add Service Images

## Quick Guide

### Step 1: Prepare Your Images
- **Size**: 300x200 pixels (or similar ratio)
- **Format**: PNG, JPG, or JPEG
- **Quality**: High resolution, professional looking

### Step 2: Replace Placeholder Files
Navigate to: `assets/images/services/`

Replace these files with your actual images:
- `general_wash.png` → Your basic car wash image
- `premium_wash.png` → Your premium service image  
- `luxury_wash.png` → Your luxury service image

### Step 3: Keep Same Filenames
**Important**: Keep the exact same filenames for automatic integration.

### Step 4: Test the App
Run `flutter run` and your images will appear on the service cards!

## Image Ideas

### General Wash
- Person washing car with basic tools
- Soap and water cleaning
- Simple, clean setup

### Premium Wash  
- Multiple cleaning tools
- Interior detailing
- Professional equipment

### Luxury Wash
- High-end car being serviced
- Premium facility
- Elegant, luxury atmosphere

## Need Help?
If images don't show up, the app will automatically fall back to icons, so your app will always work!
